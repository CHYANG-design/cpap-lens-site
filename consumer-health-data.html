Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Version 1.0　Effective date: October 29, 2026
Written for state consumer health data laws such as Washington's My Health My Data Act. It supplements the Privacy Policy.

1. What we collect and why

The health data you put in CPAP Lens (machine records, oximetry, height, weight, sleep, heart rate, oxygen saturation and your daily log) is processed only on your phone and is not sent to us. We receive consumer health data only if you choose to send it to us, for example as an email attachment. We use it only to answer you and fix problems, and delete attachments within 14 days after the issue is resolved.

2. Sources

The SD card or folder you choose, files you share from your oximeter's app, Health Connect (with your permission), what you enter in the App, and anything you send us.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or share consumer health data. The only third party that may handle it is our email provider, when you email us.

4. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask whether we hold your consumer health data, get a copy, learn who we have shared it with, withdraw consent and have it deleted. Data in the App is on your device and you can delete it in Settings. For data you have sent us, email yang@chyang.tw. If we decline a request, reply with "Appeal" in the subject line; if we deny your appeal, you may contact your state Attorney General.

C. H. Yang, individual developer, Taiwan. yang@chyang.tw
