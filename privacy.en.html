Summary
  • We run no servers. The App does not request network access and does not collect, transmit or keep any of the data you have in the App.
  • All analysis happens on your phone, and your data is stored in the App's private storage.
  • Data leaves the App only when you choose to export or share it.
  • We receive a small amount of information in only two cases: purchase details from Google Play when you buy Pro, and whatever you send us when you contact us.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

1. Scope

This policy explains how CPAP Lens (the "App") handles your data. The App is developed and maintained by C. H. Yang, an individual developer based in Taiwan ("we", "us" or the "Developer"). Please read this policy together with the Terms of Use and Disclaimer.

2. We do not collect the data you have in the App

  1. The App does not request network access and does not connect to our servers or any third-party server.
  2. The App needs no account, shows no ads and uses no analytics, tracking or crash reporting tools.
  3. We cannot access any data you have in the App.
  4. The exceptions are described in Section 7: purchase details when you buy Pro, and information you choose to send us.

4. Health Connect

  1. The App reads the data types listed above only after you allow them on the Health Connect permission screen. It never writes, changes or deletes data in Health Connect.
  2. By default Health Connect only provides data from the 30 days before you first granted access. The App reads older data of these types (for example, a height you set earlier or past weights) only if you also allow "access past data", and uses it for the same purposes listed above.
  3. Data from Health Connect is used only for features the App shows you. It is not used for advertising, is not sold or transferred to any third party, and is not used to decide credit, insurance or employment eligibility. The App's use of Health Connect data follows the Health Connect Permissions Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  4. You can withdraw access in Health Connect settings at any time. Data already read stays in the App until you delete it there.

5. Storage, retention and backup

  1. Data is stored in the App's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.
  2. Nightly data, daily summaries and daily logs are kept until you delete them. Full-night timelines, event waveforms and raw oximetry data are kept for the latest 90 days by default; you can change this in the App's storage settings.
  3. If you turn on Android system backup, nightly data, daily summaries, daily logs, user names, device serial numbers and App settings may be backed up by Android to your Google account, under Google's privacy policy. Full-night timelines, event waveforms and raw oximetry data are not included. You can turn off backup in Android settings.
  4. When you use "Back up data", the backup file is saved where you choose (for example Google Drive or your phone's Downloads folder) and is handled under that service's terms. You can encrypt the backup with a password; an unencrypted backup contains health-related data, so keep it safe.

6. Sharing

  1. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information.
  2. When you use Export or Share, the file is handed through the Android share menu to the app or service you choose (including artificial intelligence services) and is handled under the recipient's terms once it leaves the App. Exported files do not include user names or device serial numbers, but they do contain health-related data.
  3. When you choose a Google Drive folder, files are downloaded to your device by the Google Drive app under Google's privacy policy.
  4. When you buy Pro, payment is processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. The App only checks your purchase status through the Google Play Store app on your device and does not send any health-related data.

8. Your choices and rights

  • In the App's Settings, you can delete all data for a user.
  • When you uninstall the App, Android deletes all of the App's data on your device.
  • You can withdraw Health Connect, notification and folder access at any time.
  • For information we receive under Section 7, you can ask to access, correct or delete it, or ask us to stop using it, by emailing us. Additional rights for some regions are in Sections 12 and 13.

9. Security

Data in the App stays on your device; we recommend setting a screen lock to reduce the risk if your device is lost. We protect information we receive under Section 7 with reasonable safeguards, and if it is ever compromised, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.

10. Age

The App is for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children or minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. A parent or legal guardian may manage data for a minor.

11. Where information is handled

Data in the App stays on your device. Information you send us under Section 7 is received and kept by us in Taiwan, through our email provider. Taiwan's data protection law may differ from the law where you live.

12. Users in the United States

Some states have laws about consumer health data. Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy explains what we do and how to make a request. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

13. Users in Australia

You can ask to access or correct personal information we hold about you by emailing us. Information you send us is held in Taiwan (see Section 11). If you are not satisfied with our response to a complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at www.oaic.gov.au.

When this policy changes, we will update this page with a new effective date and notify you in the App of significant changes.
